What comes next is not more AI agents — it is making this one chain verifiable

30 days → 90 days → 120 days · axis linear in real days · brightness = certainty(darker = nearer and more certain) · two sets of numbers per phase: COLLECT = how much to gather，GATE = what has to be true. Fail the gate and the next phase does not start
DAY 0–30 · GATE 1

Validate the engines

No new features. Only evidence. What comes out is a test suite that can be run again.

  • 150 hand-labelled résumés (100 for tuning / 50 held out, never used for tuning）
    Sources: consented Talentbank samples plus campus recruitment, consent recorded per résumé
  • Malaysia source registry: every source with its licence, its age and its sample size
  • Eval harness plus a model and prompt registry, so any past score can be replayed

labelled résumés150
labelled claims~2,200
official sources6
GATE · quality bar
CITATION≥99%
FIELD F1≥.85
PRECISION≥.90
ROLLBACK

citation < 99% sends us back to extraction. Not one score is published in this phase。

DAY 31–90 · GATE 2

Partner beta

Real employers, and the loop turns for the first time. Scope locked to 3 role families.

  • Talentbank employer feed plus 5 employers signed for the trial
    Job descriptions and outcomes both from approved feeds — nothing scraped
  • Reason codes finalised (8–12) plus certificate verification
  • Queue autoscaling and a dead-letter queue: a failure resumes from the last good stage
COLLECT · how much
signed employers≥5
real job descriptions≥200
candidates≥300
OUTCOME≥150
GATE · quality bar
employers who return3/5
certificate parsing≥90%
salary band coverage≥80%
ROLLBACK

Under 50 outcomes and no aggregate is published — the beta extends instead. The cohort threshold is never lowered to manufacture signal.

DAY 91–120 · GATE 3

Calibrate & harden

The first time real outcomes are used to look back at the scores, and the system is made to hold.

  • Use the outcomes collected for a first calibration reading (a reading, not a conclusion)
  • Drift monitoring and a de-identified warehouse (zero PII columns)
  • HA / DR: a real recovery rehearsal in ap-southeast-5, not a documented one
COLLECT · how much
people with a follow-up result≥150
cumulative outcomes≥250
GATE · quality bar
calibration error≤10pp
DR rehearsals≥1
PII columns0
ROLLBACK

Error > 15pp pulls the numeric scores entirely. What stays is a qualitative range and the evidence list behind it.

  • ✕ Add more AI agents

  • ✕ Scrape LinkedIn / JobStreet
  • ✕ Split into microservices
  • ✕ Claim Career DNA is validated
  • ✕ Lower a threshold to get signal
Two things stated plainly

① The target numbers are proposed baselines. They need the team to confirm them before work starts and to commit them to the repository. They are not industry standards.
② Day 120 produces three months of calibration readings, not a calibrated system. Doing it properly needs 6–12 months of outcomes. That is the round after this one.
