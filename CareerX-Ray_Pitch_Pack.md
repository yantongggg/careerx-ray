## SLIDE 2 — The Problem

### Nobody fails their career loudly. They fail it quietly, over 24 months.

Career damage is **invisible while it's still fixable**, and **obvious only once it's expensive**.

Four things happen to a Malaysian professional at the same time — and none of them send a notification:

| The silent failure | What it actually looks like |
|---|---|
| **AI erosion** | 62% of your weekly tasks quietly become automatable. Nobody tells you. |
| **Salary drift** | Your +3% annual increment felt like progress. The market moved 12%. You're now RM 1.6k/month behind — **RM 19k a year, RM 58k over three years.** |
| **Skill plateau** | Your core skill hasn't grown in 14 months. The stack moved on without you. |
| **Feedback vacuum** | You get rejected. You are never told why. You repeat the same mistake for 8 applications. |

**The market data confirms this is systemic, not personal:**
- Malaysia labour force: **17.31 million** (DOSM, Mar 2026)
- **1.96 million** degree and diploma holders are in **skill-related underemployment** — 35.5% of employed graduates. Employed, but underused.
- Graduate unemployment 3.2%; overall unemployment 2.9% (517,700 people)

> Underemployment — not unemployment — is Malaysia's real career crisis. 1.96 million people have a job and still have a career problem. **Job boards cannot see them. They already have jobs.**

**The gap in the market:** every existing product tells you *where to apply*. Nothing tells you *whether you're quietly losing*.

## SLIDE 4 — The Solution

### From confusion → clarity, in one continuous loop.

We didn't build a job board with AI bolted on. We built a **medical diagnostic model for careers**.

> **X-Ray Scan → Blind Spots → Decision Lab → Prescription → Prove**

| Stage | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| **01 · X-Ray Scan** | "What's actually true about my career right now?" |
| **02 · Blind Spots** | "What can't I see — and what will it cost me?" |
| **03 · Decision Lab** | "What does each future actually look like?" |
| **04 · Prescription** | "What do I do Monday morning?" |
| **05 · Prove** | "How do I show an employer I did it?" |

**Why the medical metaphor is the whole product thesis:**
A doctor doesn't hand you a list of hospitals. They diagnose, explain, prognose, and prescribe. Every career product today hands you a list of hospitals.

**And why we didn't pick just one challenge module:** the candidate modules only become *sharp* when hiring outcomes flow back into them. We built the **Career Path Navigator, AI Career Coach, Fair Pay Engine, Living Portfolio and Life Chapter Designer** as one connected system — then wired the Employer and University tracks in as the data source that keeps them honest.

## SLIDE 5 — Candidate Module Details (1/3) · DIAGNOSE

### Every score defends itself with evidence.

**① Career X-Ray Scan** — *Onboarding → Dashboard*
- Inclusive intake: **"Start with what you have. A resume is optional."** Four evidence doors — a project, a certificate, a link (LinkedIn / Behance / Dribbble / personal site), or a resume. SPM, diploma, TVET, competition certs and class assignments all count as evidence.
- **Career Calibration** — 6 scenario questions that map answers to 6 DNA dimensions, deliberately included so the model doesn't over-reward people who happen to have a GitHub.
- 5-stage live scan: Career DNA → benchmark against **240,000+ job postings and 12,000+ career trajectories** → 50+ risk models → 4 future scenarios → prescription.
- **Output: 4 diagnostic scores** — Career Health **74/100**, AI Exposure **High**, vs Market **–14%**, Promotion Ready **65%**.
- **Every single score is clickable** and opens *"Why this score?"* → **"Evidence the AI used"** (signed positive/negative), *"What happens if nothing changes"*, and a per-metric AI confidence (91% / 86% / 88% / 79%; overall **89%**).

**② Career DNA — not a personality test, a direction map**
- 6 dimensions → one of **12 career archetypes** (Forge Beaver, Spark Fox, Crown Eagle, Signal Owl…).
- **DNA Conflict Detection** — our most original idea. We plot **Evidence** (what you actually do) against **Aspiration** (what you say you want) on the same radar, then compute `gap = aspiration − evidence` and surface every gap ≥ 15 points.
  - Result: *"You are a **Forge Beaver** transitioning toward a **Spark Fox**. This isn't confusion — it's evolution."*
  - Innovation ↑33 · Strategic ↑28 · Execution ↓27 · Technical ↓16 → **"4 Conflicts Detected"**
- **Confidence is shown per dimension**, and we mark Leadership as *Low confidence* because it is only inferred from job titles. We tell users what we don't know.

## SLIDE 8 — The WOW: The Career Intelligence Graph

### One rejection. Three systems. Same session. Nobody identified.

This is the thing no one else can build, and it runs **live on stage**.

A recruiter in our Hiring Pipeline clicks **"Reject with Feedback"** on a candidate and types the reason: *"No cloud certification."*

The system normalises it to a canonical skill — **"Cloud deployment (AWS/GCP)"** — strips the employer identity to *"a digital banking employer"*, and fans it out three ways instantly:

**→ To the candidate (personal):**
Constructive feedback instead of silence. And on their Command Center, the intelligence banner flips to a pulsing **"Live market signal"**: *"Across the Talentbank network this week, the #1 reason candidates for data roles don't move forward: missing Cloud deployment evidence — 34% of applicants share this gap."* Counter ticks **218 → 219 anonymised hiring outcomes.**

**→ To 1,247 candidates (aggregate):** the same signal, targeting similar roles.

**→ To the university (systemic, never individual):**
The dean's Pattern Alert increments the cloud cluster **18 → 19 outcomes across 6 employers**, and the Curriculum Engine injects a **live gap row** — taught 22% / needed 88%, tagged *"From live hiring signal"* — bumping Skill Gaps Detected from **12 → 13 (+1 live)**.

**→ And the loop is measured closing.** In our Hiring Outcome Signals, one cluster is green: *SQL fundamentals — 4 outcomes, **−18% this month** — "Gap closing after last semester's course update."* Outcome Loop records it end to end: *73% placement relevance revealed a cloud gap → AWS modules added to Year 3 → **next cohort cloud placement +18%, employer satisfaction 92% → 94%.***

**Privacy is enforced in the code, not the pitch deck.** Employer identity, individual identity and hiring stage are held for the university view and **never rendered** to candidates. The university layer is written to speak only in patterns: *"Individual candidates are never identified."*

> Today, a curriculum takes **4 years** to respond to the job market. We made it respond in **one session.**

# PART 2 — PROJECT SUMMARY (295 words)

**CareerX-Ray — Discover hidden career risks before they become career regrets.**

Career damage is invisible while it's still fixable and obvious only when it's expensive. In Malaysia, 1.96 million degree and diploma holders — 35.5% of employed graduates — are in skill-related underemployment. They have jobs, so job boards can't see them. Meanwhile AI quietly automates their tasks, their annual 3% increment falls further behind a market moving at 12%, and every rejection arrives without a reason.

CareerX-Ray is a Career Intelligence Layer built for those people: students deciding what to learn, fresh graduates entering a shifting market, and working professionals who don't yet know they're drifting.

We built a medical diagnostic model for careers. An **X-Ray Scan** produces four scores — Career Health, AI Exposure, salary position, promotion readiness — and every score is clickable, showing the exact evidence the AI used and its confidence. **Blind Spot Detection** surfaces five risks with a full causal chain and a market map showing which roles are shrinking, where you sit, and which growth lanes your existing skills already reach. **Decision Lab** simulates three futures year by year with salary, AI risk and promotion odds attached. **Career Prescription** converts it all into a dated 30/90-day plan projected to move Career Health from 74 to 92. The differentiator is the loop. When an employer rejects a candidate with a reason, that reason is anonymised and fans out in the same session: personal feedback to the candidate, an aggregated market signal to 1,247 similar candidates, and a live gap row on three universities' curriculum dashboards. Individuals and employers are never identified.

Twenty-three working modules across candidate, employer and university. One scan makes all three sides smarter.
